No new environment variables. Includes everything from v3.

Settings → Email
  - One sender (Sender name + Sender email) for every reminder.
  - "Reply-to by customer category": one or more reply-to addresses for each category (comma separated).
  - "Default reply-to": used for Unassigned customers and any category left blank.
  - The message window shows the reply-to picked for the customer's category; it can be changed for that one email.
  - The reply-to used is recorded in the Activity log.
